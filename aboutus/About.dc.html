Who is L'OKAWA

L'OKAWA Tours and Travel (U) Ltd is a Uganda-based travel company. We plan tour packages, safari adventures, hotel bookings, airport transfers and group trips across Uganda, Kenya, Rwanda and Tanzania.

Every journey is built around your pace, your budget and the wildlife you most want to see, and looked after by people who grew up on these roads.
